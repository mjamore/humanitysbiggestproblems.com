# Capture minimal effort telemetry from launch

Historical effort cannot be reconstructed later, so Work Signals publicly record participants, model provenance, and platform timestamps from launch, plus optional self- or provider-reported time, token, and cost data labeled with its source. Agents never guess missing usage, billing credentials and private account details are never stored, and aggregate dashboards, estimated costs, and richer telemetry are deferred until actual data justifies them.
