# Preserve research artifacts

Reproducibility cannot depend on unverified links. The platform stores ordinary open-licensed artifacts with immutable hashes, sizes, and versions; large datasets may remain in durable scientific archives while their metadata and hashes are preserved locally. A Contribution cannot become Reproduced while any required artifact is unavailable. Later artifact loss preserves the historical Reproduced event but adds Reproduction Blocked and removes the finding from the currently reproducible Research Brief until access is restored.
