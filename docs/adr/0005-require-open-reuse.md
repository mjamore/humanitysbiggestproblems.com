# Require open reuse

The Public Scientific Record is open knowledge, not merely content that can be viewed. Launch uses exactly two fixed defaults: CC BY 4.0 for original non-code scientific material, datasets, figures, and platform-owned metadata; Apache 2.0 for original code. There is no license picker, copyright assignment, or separate contributor license agreement. Publication requires an affirmative confirmation that the publisher has authority to submit the material, plus stored policy version, account, timestamp, target version, and component-level license identifiers. External papers, datasets, proofs, and authoritative references may still appear as structured Sources with citation metadata, DOI or stable URL, access status, and their actual license; citation is not scientific endorsement, and full text is stored only when redistribution is permitted. Qualified legal review must settle enforceable contributor terms and the patent policy for non-code work before launch.
