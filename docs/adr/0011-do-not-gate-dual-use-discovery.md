# Do not gate dual-use discovery

The platform does not reject, quarantine, or suppress qualifying mathematical, natural-science, or computer-science work because of its possible harmful or dual-use applications. Open publication of scientific truth takes precedence over attempts to control downstream use; potential misuse alone is not a moderation criterion. Site Moderators may still remove objective abuse such as spam, harassment, doxxing, malware, stolen private material, or content the platform is legally required to remove.
