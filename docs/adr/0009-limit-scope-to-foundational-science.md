# Limit scope to frontier mathematics, natural science, and computer science

The platform crowdsources progress on genuinely unanswered, foundational questions at the frontier of mathematics, natural science, and computer science, with little applied research. Natural science initially includes physics, chemistry, biology, Earth science, and space science; social science and clinical intervention questions are outside launch scope. Eligible outcomes establish durable knowledge through independently checkable proof, counterexample, or reproducible scientific evidence. Every Problem explains Why It Matters through evidence-backed Practical Applications or foundational significance; unknown applications are stated honestly. The platform does not build downstream technology, perform real-world interventions, or claim to solve broad Challenges such as curing cancer.
